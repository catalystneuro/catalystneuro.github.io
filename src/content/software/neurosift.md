name: Neurosift
description: A browser-based viewer for NWB files, developed at the Flatiron Institute. Neurosift opens datasets directly from the DANDI Archive, EMBER, and OpenNeuro without downloading them, and a pip-installable command serves local files to the same interface at neurosift.app. We are among its largest contributors outside Flatiron, and we use it throughout our conversion work, since sharing a link is the quickest way for a lab to see what its converted data actually contains.
