name: Neurosift
description: A browser-based viewer for NWB files, developed at the Flatiron Institute. Neurosift opens datasets directly from the DANDI Archive, EMBER, and OpenNeuro without downloading them, and renders what it finds as interactive views, including spike raster plots, peristimulus time histograms aligned to trial events, two-photon imaging series, and pose estimation superimposed on the behavioral video. A pip-installable command serves local files to the same interface at neurosift.app. We are among its largest contributors outside Flatiron, and we use it throughout our conversion work, since sharing a link is the quickest way for a lab to see what its converted data actually contains.
