NYU Libraries funded CatalystNeuro to help two NYU neuroscience labs convert their data to NWB and publish it on the DANDI Archive. The project was coordinated by the Libraries' Data Services department and ran in 2024 and 2025. A second goal was to train Libraries staff in standardizing and publishing neurophysiology data, so they took part throughout the process.

The two labs brought a broad mix of data. From rats performing a high-throughput Bpod task, we converted behavior, fiber photometry with optogenetics, Neuropixels recordings with Kilosort and Phy results, and DeepLabCut pose estimation, organized as four projects. From mice using sound to guide skilled movements, we converted silicon probe and Neuropixels recordings, behavior, optogenetic suppression, intrinsic signal imaging, video with SLEAP pose estimation, and audio. Each lab has an open-source conversion package and notebooks showing how to read and stream the files. The published Dandisets include 001169, the data behind the preprint "Distinct value computations support rapid sequential decisions".
