The Simons Foundation supported CatalystNeuro through the Simons Collaboration on the Global Brain (SCGB) to help neurophysiology labs adopt Neurodata Without Borders (NWB). The work began in July 2019, the same month we started nwb-conversion-tools, the predecessor of NeuroConv, and continued into 2024. The 2022 eLife paper describing the NWB ecosystem acknowledges the Simons Foundation for its support of CatalystNeuro.

Over those five years we wrote conversion pipelines for sixteen labs and collaborations, covering mice, rats, macaques, zebrafish, Drosophila, and zebra finches. The data spanned Neuropixels, Utah array, tetrode, and V-Probe recordings, two-photon and head-mounted calcium imaging, fiber photometry, optogenetics, and behavior recorded as video, pose estimation, virtual reality, and task events. Several projects needed new NWB extensions for lab-specific metadata, and a few included graphical tools for editing metadata or custom widgets for viewing the converted files. Many of the datasets were published on the DANDI Archive, and the conversions from this project link to thirteen Dandisets.
