Aligning Science Across Parkinson's (ASAP) is a research initiative, managed by the Coalition for Aligning Science and implemented by the Michael J. Fox Foundation, that funds the multi-institution teams of its Collaborative Research Network. In 2023 ASAP launched a pilot with CatalystNeuro to build pipelines that convert data from these labs to NWB and archive it on the DANDI Archive. The work has continued since.

We have worked with fourteen labs in the United States and Sweden, on data from mice and macaques. The data include fiber photometry, extracellular recordings in behaving animals, patch clamp and imaging in ex vivo slices, two-photon, mesoscopic, and widefield imaging, EEG, and fast-scan cyclic voltammetry, usually alongside behavior, video, and pose estimation. For each lab we write an open-source conversion package, train the lab in using NWB, and write example notebooks showing how to stream and analyze the data from DANDI, most of which are contributed to the DANDI example-notebooks repository. So far the work has produced thirteen Dandisets, ten of them with published versions, including recordings from macaques before and after MPTP-induced parkinsonism and fiber photometry of dopamine release as mice develop compulsive behavior.
