description: "We are looking for a Research Software Engineer to help neuroscience labs convert their data to Neurodata Without Borders (NWB) and publish it on the DANDI Archive. Each project starts with a lab's own acquisition systems, file formats, and analysis outputs, and ends with a standardized, well-documented dataset that others can reuse. The work is roughly evenly split between building conversion pipelines for specific labs and improving the open-source tools those pipelines are built on, including NeuroConv, PyNWB, and the NWB Inspector. You would join a small, fully remote team that works closely with each other and with the research groups we support."

responsibilities:
  - "Build and maintain NWB conversion pipelines for electrophysiology, optical physiology, behavior, and related experimental data"
  - "Meet with lab members to understand their experiments, data formats, and metadata, and translate that into accurate NWB files"
  - "Prepare datasets for publication on the DANDI Archive and help labs through the upload and validation process"
  - "Contribute features, bug fixes, and documentation to open-source projects such as NeuroConv, PyNWB, HDMF, and the NWB Inspector"
  - "Review code from teammates and outside contributors, and write tests for the code you contribute"
  - "Manage several projects at once and keep labs informed of progress and open questions"
requirements:
  - "Strong Python skills, including experience with NumPy and working with large scientific datasets"
  - "Experience with a scientific data format such as HDF5, Zarr, or a vendor format used in neuroscience"
  - "A background in neuroscience, biomedical engineering, or a related field, or substantial experience working with neuroscience data"
  - "Familiarity with Git, GitHub, and collaborative open-source development"
  - "Clear written and spoken communication in English, and comfort working directly with researchers"
  - "Ability to work independently in a remote team and overlap several hours a day with European working hours"
  - "Experience with NWB, DANDI, SpikeInterface, or related tools is a plus but not required"
